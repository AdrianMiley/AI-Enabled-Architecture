1. Pull out your current strategy deck.
   • When was it last updated? By whom? Are they still there?
2. Pick your top 3 use cases. Now ask:
   • Did business request these, or did we decide they'd be valuable?
3. Talk to 5 actual users. Not stakeholders. Users.
   • Ask what would save them time or help them make better decisions.
4. Check your user research.
   • If it exists and you dismissed it as "not innovative," revisit it. Real problems = real value.

   • It's a measurable impact that compounds.
